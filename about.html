Our Program

About the Program

Grades 4–8 soccer at Ashland Christian School. Our program is an extension of what ACS has done for 50 years: partnering with families to grow the whole student, on the field and off.

What we're about

Rooted in the ACS mission

Ashland Christian School exists to prepare students both academically and spiritually to take their place in God's world. Soccer is one more place we get to live that out. On the field it means developing godly character, competing with effort and integrity, and honoring God in how we play, win or lose.

"Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord." — Colossians 3:23
How we coach

Development first

We use the US Soccer grassroots “play-practice-play” approach: small-sided games, lots of touches, and real development for every player. We care about growth over the scoreboard. Kids who work hard and love the game get better, and that's the win.

Character

How we carry ourselves

We build the same godly character ACS teaches in the classroom into how we play: respect for teammates, opponents, refs, and coaches; effort every rep; and being the kind of teammate others want to play with. We compete hard and we do it with humility.

Families

Partnering with parents

ACS has partnered with parents for five decades, and this program runs the same way. We'll keep communication open and simple through Remind so you always know what's happening, and we'd love to have families at games cheering the Lions on.

What we expect

Team standards

  • Show up and give your best effort at practice and games
  • Respect teammates, opponents, officials, and coaches
  • Represent ACS and Christ well, on the field and on the sideline
  • Communicate early if you'll miss a practice or game
