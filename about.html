Our Program

About the Program

Grades 4–8 soccer at Ashland Christian School. Our program is an extension of what ACS has done for 50 years: partnering with families to grow the whole student, on the field and off.

What we're about

Rooted in the ACS mission

Ashland Christian School exists to prepare students both academically and spiritually to take their place in God's world. Soccer is one more place we get to live that out. On the field it means developing Christ-like character, competing with effort and integrity, and honoring God in how we play, win or lose.

"Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord." — Colossians 3:23
How we coach

Development first

At its core, our approach is simple: kids get better by playing. We build practice around the game itself, lots of touches, small-sided games, and real decisions on the ball, instead of standing in lines. We value effort, creativity, and steady growth over the scoreboard. Every player develops, and that's the win.

Character

More than the game

Our program is designed to develop more than physical skill. We aim to shape discipline, teamwork, and Christ-like character in every player. Soccer isn't just about competition, it's a platform for spiritual growth and personal development, both on and off the field. We want our players building confidence, honoring God, and representing ACS with integrity in everything they do.

Families

Partnering with parents

ACS has partnered with parents for five decades, and this program runs the same way. We'll keep communication open and simple through Remind so you always know what's happening, and we'd love to have families at games cheering the Lions on.

What we expect

Team standards

  • Show up and give your best effort at practice and games
  • Respect teammates, opponents, officials, and coaches
  • Represent ACS and Christ well, on the field and on the sideline
  • Communicate early if you'll miss a practice or game

FAQ

Common Questions

When will we get uniforms?

We usually hand those out a week or so before the first game. Uniforms will need to be returned at the end of the season.

How much playing time will my child get?

We value developing players over winning games. Older kids will get more playing time, but everyone plays in every game, as long as they show up and keep up their academic and behavioral standard at school.

How can I get involved?

Every game has volunteer opportunities, from running the scoreboard to selling refreshments. Contact the school office to find a spot that works for you.
