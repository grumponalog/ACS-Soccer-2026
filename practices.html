Regular Season

Practices

Once school starts we move into the regular weekly practice schedule.

Required

Physical on file before you practice

Players cannot participate in practice until a completed sports physical is turned in to Mrs. LaGoy and the player has been cleared by a physician.

Practice Schedule

Mon / Tue / Thu / Fri · 3:15–5:00 PM

Practices run these four days each week once the regular season starts.

  • Tuesday
  • Thursday
  • Friday
What to bring

Every practice

Come to each practice ready to go with:

  • Cleats
  • Shin guards
  • Socks (tall enough to hold the shin guards)
  • Water bottle
  • Athletic shorts
  • A t-shirt
Good to know

Every practice is on the schedule

All practice dates and times are set and live on the team calendar. Practices begin the week of August 17 (no practice Monday the 17th, that's back-to-school night) and don't run on game days.
